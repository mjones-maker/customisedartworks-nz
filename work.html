Colour-reveal engraving

A different technique from the line work above: I paint the canvas the colours I'm after, seal it under a black top layer, then laser-etch the image to reveal the colour beneath. Two-colour pieces are the hardest — and every canvas comes out one of a kind, even from the same image.

The Auckland skyline and its reflection — Sky Tower, the harbour, the city — painted and then revealed in silver on black. A piece of home you can hold: on an office wall, or packed in a suitcase by someone who's headed overseas.

Featured

Auckland, in layers

Tāmaki Makaurau built up from the coastline: every suburb and street laser-cut from ply and stacked in relief, the harbour poured in blue, the exact coordinates engraved along the base. It can be made for any place that means something: a hometown, a harbour, a farewell for someone leaving it behind.
